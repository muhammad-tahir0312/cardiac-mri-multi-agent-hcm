## Working project

External validation of an auditable cardiac magnetic resonance imaging pipeline integrating automated segmentation, deterministic ventricular quantification, guideline-grounded report generation and failure-aware quality control.

## Purpose of the requested data

The requested controlled dataset would be used as an independent, locked external test cohort. It would not be used for public redistribution or commercial purposes. Model thresholds and evaluation rules would be fixed before analysis of the external cohort.

## Primary objective

To evaluate patient-level LV cavity, LV myocardium and RV cavity segmentation performance from predicted masks on an independent CMR cohort, together with the resulting ventricular-volume and ejection-fraction errors.

## Minimum useful data

- De-identified short-axis cine CMR covering the ventricles from base to apex.
- Full temporal sequence or identified end-diastolic and end-systolic frames.
- LV cavity, LV myocardium and RV cavity reference contours at ED and ES, where available.
- Pixel spacing, slice thickness/gap and orientation.
- Scanner vendor, field strength and acquisition site or anonymized site identifier.
- Patient-level pseudonymous identifier to prevent phase/slice leakage between splits.
- Diagnostic label and description of how the reference diagnosis was established, where permitted.

Clinical reports and additional sequences such as LGE, T1/T2 mapping or perfusion would be optional and would be used only under a separately specified and scientifically supported endpoint.

## Governance position

This is currently a preliminary feasibility enquiry. No controlled data will be requested for transfer until the appropriate Universiti Malaya ethics or jurisdiction determination, custodian approval, institutional agreement and secure-storage requirements have been completed. The study is seeking data access rather than asking the data custodians or medical doctors to join the research team.
